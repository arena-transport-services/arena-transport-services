About Us

A can-do courier company

Arena Transport Services Pty Ltd is a Sydney-based business that has delivered express courier services across the Sydney Metropolitan Area since early 2000.

We offer on-demand courier deliveries, taxi trucks from 1 to 12 tonnes, fast 1–4 hour courier services, and permanent runs or exclusive hire.

Core Focus

We run as an owner-operated business, with daily set runs for companies, managed day to day. That keeps the operation smart, clean and simple, built on these principles:

  • Direct interaction with customers
  • Proof of delivery (POD) available within 48 hours
  • Weekly billing with detailed records
